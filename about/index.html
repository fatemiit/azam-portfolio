Product Designer

Hi, I’m Azam.

I’m a product designer with five years of work on fintech, e-commerce and streaming products.

Most of my projects start with something messy, like a confusing flow or a drop in a funnel, and end with a screen that’s simpler to use and easier to measure.

Get in touch

I like to understand people before I design for them.

I’m curious about how people make decisions. So before I open Figma, I ask a lot of questions: who is this for, what are they trying to do, and where do they get stuck? The answer usually points to a simpler design than the one I first had in mind.

Before design, I studied economics. It still shows up in my work. When a number drops, I look for the incentive or the step behind it.

A few things I keep coming back to.

Ask before drawing

I’d rather spend a day understanding the problem than a week polishing the wrong idea.

Keep what matters visible

Trading, payments and checkout are places where one confusing step costs real money. I try to remove steps without hiding information people need.

Check it against the business

A design has to work for the user, make sense for the business and be something the team can actually build.

Zoom in, then zoom out

I care about the small moments in a flow, but I keep checking them against the whole journey.
